# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective Greenwood Homes clients evaluating whether to entrust the company with a custom home or related residential construction project.

## Product Purpose

Present Greenwood Homes' verified body of work, experience, services, team, and client record clearly enough that a prospective client chooses to begin a project conversation. The homepage's primary action should be the most direct verified path toward becoming a client: contacting Greenwood Homes.

## Positioning

Greenwood Homes combines a portfolio of more than 50 custom homes with licensed operations across California, Nevada, Hawaii, Idaho, and Utah, an established history since 1998, recognized craftsmanship, comprehensive building services, experienced regional teams, and repeated client evidence of integrity, communication, collaboration, and follow-through.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be planning a new custom home, renovation, or related residential project in Lake Tahoe/Truckee, Hawaii, Idaho, or Utah. They are making a high-trust, high-investment decision and need evidence that Greenwood can deliver both an exceptional residence and a well-managed building experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Greenwood Homes offers custom home building, remodels and renovations, pre-construction budgeting and scheduling, construction management, sustainability expertise, and post-construction support.
- The homepage must use only claims, names, locations, licenses, services, testimonials, awards, project details, and destinations verified in `proof.md`.
- The downloaded files catalogued in `public/images/manifest.json` are the source visual library.
- The primary conversion path is the verified Greenwood Homes contact destination.
- Do not invent claims, awards, testimonials, locations, services, destinations, or other commercial facts.
- Do not reuse the old Greenwood/Relume screenshots, architecture, or placeholder-template visual system.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Greenwood Homes name and supplied logo asset.
- Preserve the factual character of the source voice: assured, professional, collaborative, and grounded in completed work.
- Treat this as a replacement visual world, not a refinement of an incumbent site.

## Evidence on Hand

`proof.md` is the factual source of truth and organizes seven proof types:

1. Licensed and established builder
2. Proven portfolio of custom homes
3. Award-winning craftsmanship
4. Client satisfaction and trust
5. Comprehensive building services
6. Experienced leadership team
7. Client-centered philosophy

It also contains verified external destinations. `public/images/manifest.json` records 55 downloaded source images, including the Greenwood Homes logo, completed homes, regional landscapes, team portraits, award marks, and editorial imagery. No unsupported evidence may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the strongest differentiating proof, then use all seven proof types to support the decision.
- Make completed work and specific evidence more prominent than generic luxury language.
- Turn trust into a visible sequence: established credentials, demonstrated craft, capable people, collaborative process, and client testimony.
- Keep the path from conviction to a project conversation obvious.
- Preserve regional breadth without making the company feel impersonal or diffuse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should remain usable with keyboard navigation, respect reduced-motion preferences, provide meaningful text alternatives for imagery, and maintain readable contrast and type across desktop and mobile.
