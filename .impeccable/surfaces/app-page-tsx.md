# Greenwood Homes homepage

- Scope: `app/page.tsx`, Persuade mode.
- Audience: prospective custom-home and residential-construction clients evaluating Greenwood Homes.
- Job: establish desire and trust, then begin a project conversation through the verified contact destination.
- Proof: give each proof type its own concrete section or artifact. Show exact licenses, completed work, exact awards, a full verified testimonial, the service sequence, named team members, and collaboration through the process; do not summarize these into a generic proof component.
- Constraints: `proof.md` is factual authority; `public/images/manifest.json` is visual authority; no invented claims, awards, testimonials, services, locations, or destinations; no sidebar navigation.
- Direction: Builder’s Pattern Book — evidence organized as tactile vellum construction plates over real completed work.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/proof-cabinet.png` (The Proof Cabinet).
- Memorable moment: one wide charcoal thesis field meets a monumental verified home photograph, with the fired-clay contact tab pinned at their seam.
- User steer: remove the proof cabinet and vertical leaves, move all seven proof types into a separate evidence ledger, widen the left hero field, preserve the open top navigation, and use the original Greenwood Homes logo.

## Implementation fidelity

- Component grammar: open editorial fields, ruled evidence records, and pinned tabs; no rounded cards or pills.
- Corners: nearly square, with clipped tab notches where the material calls for them.
- Lines: fine charcoal rules and exact drafting strokes; no generic boxed grid.
- Elevation: low, soft offset shadows for physical paper sheets; brass pins carry localized depth.
- Type: compressed, sturdy display lettering for headlines; calm humanist sans for copy; narrow uppercase notation only for indexes and measurements.
- Responsive: the hero stacks thesis above photography on mobile; the evidence ledger becomes a linear reading sequence without sideways headline or sidebar UI.
